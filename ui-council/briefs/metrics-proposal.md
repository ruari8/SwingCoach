# Brief: propose a design-review rubric for SwingCoach

You are on a design review council for SwingCoach, an iPhone golf coaching app. You are read-only: do not edit, create, commit or push any file in the repository. Reply in your final message only.

## The app

SwingCoach films golf swings at the range, keeps them in a library, and coaches down-the-line swings on the phone. Key screens:

- **Library**: swings grouped by session, with a session review button.
- **Session review**: the session's theme (the fault that showed most), pictures of it at its worst and best, a dot per swing, a trend chart.
- **Review player**: the swing video, paused on the coach's first finding with a drawing over the golfer, a step card ("Shaft steepens coming down", step 1 of 2), a P1–P10 position strip, and a side tool rail (Info, Speed, Draw, Positions, Drawings, Full screen).
- **All checks**: every measured check with its zone and level.
- **Capture**: the camera screen. Auto mode detects and saves swings hands-free and shows a coach chip and a recap card every few swings; Manual mode records on tap.
- **Settings**.

The player and camera screens are always dark; the Library, Settings and sheets follow the system appearance.
