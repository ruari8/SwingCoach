# UI council rubric (frozen 20 Nov 2026, round 0)

Merged from the metric proposals of GPT Sol 6.2, Opus 5.5 and Fable 5.2. Gemini Neon 4's proposal was still running when the rubric was frozen. Each metric is scored 1–10 with one decimal, from screenshots at phone size, in light and dark. Do not change this file after round 0.

| # | Key | Metric | What it measures | 10 looks like | 5 looks like |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `typography` | Typography | Type scale, weights, numerals, line breaks and the legibility of the smallest text. | Three or four sizes with clear roles, tabular numbers in lists and charts, no awkward wraps or truncation, and even the smallest caption is comfortable. | Readable, but too many sizes or weights, captions under 11 pt, or ragged wraps and ellipses. |
| 2 | `hierarchy` | Visual hierarchy | Whether each screen has one obvious first read (the finding, the theme, the record state), then a clear second and third. | In one second the eye lands on what matters and everything else is visibly subordinate. | Three or four elements compete at similar weight; the order has to be worked out by reading. |
| 3 | `spacing` | Spacing and density | Margins, grid, vertical rhythm, safe areas, and how much each screen shows at once against what it pushes one tap deeper. | Edges line up, gaps come from a small set, the default view is calm, and detail is clearly reachable but not shown up front. | Generally tidy, but one-off gaps, crowded edges, or a screen that shows everything at once. |
| 4 | `color` | Color and contrast | A limited, deliberate palette, and contrast of text, controls and status over surfaces and over real footage. | One accent and one fixed status set; everything essential holds up over bright grass and sky as well as dark surfaces. | Pleasant colors, but some controls fade into the footage, or the palette has drifted into several unrelated accents. |
| 5 | `consistency` | Consistency | Whether the same component, term, symbol and color means and looks the same on every screen. | A recap card on Capture and a card in the Library are visibly one family; a level badge, P-marker or "in zone" green means one thing everywhere. | Recognizably related parts that have drifted in padding, radius, font, icon style or wording. |
| 6 | `clarity` | Clarity of message and controls | Whether the coaching message says what happened and where in plain golfer language, and whether controls say what they do. | A short phrase names the fault and the moment; the next action is obvious; no icon needs guessing. | Correct but technical or wordy copy, or several icon-only controls whose meaning has to be guessed. |
| 7 | `accessibility` | Accessibility and range use | Tap targets, contrast ratios, text size, color never the only signal, and whether Capture's state reads from 2–3 m in daylight. | Every target is 44 pt or more, text meets WCAG AA, status has a shape or word as well as a color, and Auto's state is legible from a tripod. | Targets meet the minimum but are clustered, some text is low contrast, or status relies on hue or tiny text. |
| 8 | `subject` | Swing visibility | How much of the player and camera screens the golfer keeps, and whether chrome covers the body, club or ball. | The golfer and club are fully visible; controls sit at the edges and never cover hands, club head or feet. | The video is visibly secondary, or a card, rail or strip covers part of the swing. |
| 9 | `overlays` | Drawing legibility | Whether the coach's drawings over the video read clearly against real range backgrounds and look intentional. | Crisp strokes with a halo, one color meaning, attached to the body part they describe, no colliding labels. | Visible but thin, blending into grass or sky in places, or labels that collide. |
| 10 | `data` | Data and measurement readability | Values, zones, levels, swing dots and trend charts: can they be read without a legend? | Each check shows where the swing sits against the good zone at a glance; charts have a labelled better side. | Values and ranges present but need decoding; meaning carried by zone color alone. |
| 11 | `state` | State and confidence | Whether mode, current step, selection, pending reading and "measured but not judged" each look distinct. | Every state has a distinct, calm treatment; nothing pending or unjudged can be mistaken for a verdict. | States exist but look alike, or uncertainty is greyed with no reason given. |
| 12 | `polish` | Appearance and platform polish | Whether light and dark both look designed, the always-dark player sits well beside system screens, and the app uses iOS conventions with care. | Both appearances look finished; first-party-quality sheets, materials, SF Symbols and safe areas. | One appearance is the afterthought, or off-grid spacing, mixed radii and mismatched icon weights. |
| 13 | `overall` | Overall | A senior designer's overall judgment of the UI, against the best apps in the category. | It stands next to the best in the category without looking cheaper, and has a recognizable character of its own. | Clean and competent but generic, or good in parts and rough in others. |

## How references are scored

The two reference apps are scored from their App Store screenshots: judge only the UI inside the phone frames, not the marketing banners. Where a metric's state isn't shown (for example dark mode), score from what the visible UI implies and say so in one line.
