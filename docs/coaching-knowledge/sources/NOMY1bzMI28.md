[Original video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NOMY1bzMI28) · TPI · 1:41:43 · published 25 August 2026

Greg Rose and Dave Phillips assess Brennan with his established team. They connect interview findings, physical screening, power tests, video, force plates and 3D motion to iron setup, bunker entry and putting alignment. They largely preserve his driver and make shorter-driver work optional.

This source provides a useful decision example. The player's phrase “hanging back” starts as a hypothesis. TPI asks which ball-flight problem matters, compares clubs, checks the evidence and discovers that his existing interpretation of “get left” includes extra travel before pushing. The selected iron instruction addresses pelvis setup and early backswing drift. It does not simply ask for more chest movement left.

## Details future coaching must preserve

The plumb-bob reference is **pelvis centre relative to stance centre**. During teach-back, Brennan initially describes it relative to the ball and Rose corrects him. A pelvis-centre projection is also not a measured whole-body centre of mass.

The “get left” cue changes during the session. Early bunker instructions can sound strongly lead-sided; later TPI reduces excessive left lean and uses a more balanced starting feel. Driver stays largely unchanged while iron setup changes. The optional shorter-driver trial remains optional even after praise.

The transcript also has inconsistent brief setup summaries. The structured record logs those spans and follows the repeated detailed instruction and explicit final correction. Physical screening remains assessed context. No body-handicap score, phone-derived mobility diagnosis or clinical recommendation is produced.
