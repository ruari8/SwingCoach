## What this lesson contributes

This is a useful first case because Chris repeatedly explains both his diagnosis and why he declines to add other instructions. Jerome reports returning after an arm injury and trying to turn more. Chris identifies an inside, closed takeaway and excessive rotation/arm depth, connects those to the observed pushes and hooks, and makes the backswing the active focus.

At [52:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HQNKUS_BSGY&t=3164s), his decision procedure is explicit: check the agreed width, top position and face first. Consider a downswing change only if those checks are satisfactory and the unwanted shape remains. At [53:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HQNKUS_BSGY&t=3222s), he limits Jerome to two linked backswing cues. This directly supports storing a larger knowledge library while presenting a small assignment.

The intervention changes as Jerome struggles to reproduce it. A staged takeaway/top/soft-shot exercise becomes an outward movement feel; later, two physical rehearsals provide alternative ways to find the same arm structure. These belong to one intervention family. They should not become four simultaneous drills for the golfer.

The sequence is not a monotonic numerical improvement, and the recalled baseline appears later in the edit. Do not manufacture a tidy before/after trajectory from it.

Jerome applies the feels in two edited on-course examples and reports par on each, including improvement over a previous triple bogey on the second hole. Those examples support recording application and reported satisfaction. They do not isolate the swing intervention's effect or prove retention. ## Implications for future analysis

Candidate observations are clubhead relative to hands near P2, arm width/depth and shaft direction near P4, and comparison with the same golfer's reviewed rehearsal. Appropriate camera alignment, club visibility and definitions are still needed before numeric thresholds or automated diagnoses are justified.

The strongest selection rule is to preserve a working part of the swing while testing the stated cause. This video gives explicit examples of withholding release and pressure-shift instructions. Those negative decisions belong in the library alongside the drills.

There is no formal TPI body screen in this source. The injury story should remain source context; the app cannot infer a physical restriction or body handicap from it.
