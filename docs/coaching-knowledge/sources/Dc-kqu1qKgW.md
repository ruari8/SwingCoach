# Alex Clapp: forearm rotation timing

[Forearm rotation: takeaway timing and trail-arm structure](https://www.instagram.com/p/Dc-kqu1qKgW/) | Alex Clapp Golf Coach / alexclappgolf | Published 20260907 | 01:31

2 cases linked to 5 evidence spans. Full transcript read; visual review method and limits recorded below. Source checked, not independently coach-validated.

This lesson preserves a timing distinction: early rotation can take the club inside, while suppressing later rotation can leave the student in an unwanted arm/club structure. Two teaching variants address the same focus: tactile feedback above the right elbow, and an upright rotation–turn–bend comparison.
