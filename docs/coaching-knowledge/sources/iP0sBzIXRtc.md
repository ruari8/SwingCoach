## What this source adds

Danny wants better flight control and describes handle drag, lag and late release. Cowen works through right-side loading, cross-handed rehearsal and normal-grip mini shots. The first improved flight does not end the lesson. Later shots go left, thin and right for different reasons in Cowen’s assessment, so he changes the task.

This makes a useful decision sequence. Continued turning helps one error, but too much body pace produces another. Calm motion then becomes deceleration. Fade-finish imagery, a shorter task and low wind-shot intent are successive ways to recover the intended coordination.
