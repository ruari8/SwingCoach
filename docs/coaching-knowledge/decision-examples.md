# Coaching decisions illustrated by the sources

These examples show what the library adds beyond a collection of tips. Each linked report leads to timestamps, inspected frames and structured cases. This is a reading guide to extracted coach reasoning, not an implemented or validated diagnosis algorithm.

## Prerequisites and follow-up

The shallowing tutorial explicitly assumes suitable setup, alignment and backswing, and excludes an unresolved open face. Those written conditions are part of the record even though its short video mostly shows the drill. Retrieving the demonstration without the accompanying caption would lose the selection logic.

[Kyle's two-month progression](sources/Dcthf1xDWe9.md), C01–03, records the teaching order as grip/setup, takeaway/top, then transition. The later swing is an iron on the range and the earlier swing a driver on the course. This provides a reported progression and visible examples, but cannot isolate the effect of each change or establish a same-club performance gain.

[Ryan Ruffels's practice session](sources/vGGIn2IAW-Q.md), C04–05, distinguishes aided technical practice from target-focused execution without the aids. Store that transfer check alongside a drill rather than treating a successful rehearsal as the final outcome.

## What future matching must retain

For a candidate case, retrieve the actual observation, its camera or instrument context, the coach's stated reasoning, prerequisites, the selected action and the available result. Keep reported misses separate from measured ball flight, desired annotation lines separate from actual positions, and coach demonstrations separate from student retests.

Choose one current focus, normally one cue or action and at most two compatible cues. Reassess the response before advancing to a later stage. The library's proposed app reassessment notes are explicitly separate from tests actually shown in the source.

The collection is a starting evidence base. It has not yet demonstrated that SwingCoach can identify these cases reliably from phone video or improve a golfer's handicap.
