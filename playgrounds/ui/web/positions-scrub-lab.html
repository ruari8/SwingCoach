P positions, round 2

Same trimmed clip (5.7 s), every frame drawn as a tick. Bars are phone scrubber width (346 pt). Drag any of them; all three follow. Drag your finger well above or below a bar to scrub at ¼ speed.

1 · Scrub feel

Each tick is a frame. Where ticks bunch up, one point of drag skips several frames; where they spread out, the scrubber jumps from frame to frame. Switch the clip to 240 fps to see what Auto clips do.

Clip
At 30 fps, Even does what you feared: P6→P7 is 2 frames spread over 35 pt, so it jumps 17 pt per frame. At 240 fps (what Auto records) it flips: the downswing feels fine, but P9→P10 packs 384 frames into 35 pt. Time is the same everywhere, but P5–P9 sit inside about 16 pt, too tight to aim at. Balanced sits between them. No spacing gives frame-level control over 1,368 frames on a 346 pt bar, so ¼-speed fine scrub and the frame-step buttons are needed whichever we pick. So: keep the scrubber on Time and let the pose strip handle position jumps.

2 · A + D hybrid

Pose strip for jumping, time scrubber for frames, tool rail for tools. The lock stays in the transport row.

  • Strip jumps, scrubber scrubs. Dragging across the strip moves in whole positions, so the 2-frame P5→P6 gap and the 48-frame P9→P10 gap feel the same: one notch. Frame-level work stays on the scrubber.
  • Scrubber dots mark where each P sits in real time. Drag above the bar for ¼ speed through the downswing.
  • Rail: labels, draw, speed, positions on/off, info. Lock and frame steps stay in the transport row; star or delete stay top right.
  • Left-handed mirrors the rail, its labels, the transport row and the pose glyphs. P1→P10 still reads left to right, because it's time order.
  • Setting: one Handedness choice in Settings drives this. A separate tool-side override can come later if left-handers who hold the phone in their right hand ask for it.
  • Draw: lines show on every frame, or pin one to the current P (blue). Try a line at P1, then tap P6.
