  • Transport is centred again: the time and the padlock each get a 58 pt slot, as in the app today.
  • Scrubber uses Balanced spacing with the app's own track, thumb and 02.5/05.6 badge. Positions snap within 7 pt. On a P, the badge adds its name. Drag your finger well above the bar for ¼ speed.
  • Tools, top to bottom: fold, info, speed, draw, positions. The column starts one gap under the star and uses the same 38 pt circles and 10 pt spacing. In Auto it takes the empty top-right slot.
  • Fold bubble: ⌃ folds the tools up into it, ⌄ drops them back down. Open is the default. On reopening, the names show for about 1.5 s and then fade.
  • Left side is only ever the user's choice: drag the bubble across and it docks under Back. The side is remembered. Right is the default.
